Pulse

Pulse is a private, single-user app. It reads the owner's own Fitbit data through the Google Health API to give personal health summaries. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Pulse only reads data with read-only access that the owner grants through Google sign-in. Data is stored privately for the owner's own use. It is never sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
